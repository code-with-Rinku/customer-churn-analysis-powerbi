# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis – Power BI & SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **telecom customer churn** using **SQL and Microsoft Power BI**.
The objective is to understand customer churn patterns, identify high-risk customer segments, and generate meaningful business insights that can help a telecom company improve customer retention and reduce churn.
The project includes SQL-based data analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPIs, charts, and slicers.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **SQL** – Data analysis and querying
* **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and visualization
* **DAX** – Measures and calculations
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **Excel/CSV** – Data source
* **GitHub** – Project documentation and version control

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project analyzes telecom customer churn across several dimensions, including:

* Churn by gender
* Churn by contract type
* Churn by payment method
* Churn by internet service
* Churn by senior citizen status
* Churn by tenure
* Churn by customer services
* Monthly charges and churn
* Customer service usage

## 💡 Key Insights

* Customers with **month-to-month contracts** have a higher tendency to churn.
* Customers with **higher monthly charges** show a greater likelihood of churn.
* Churn varies across different **internet service types**.
* **Customer tenure** is an important factor in customer retention.
* Different additional services show differences between customers who churn and those who remain.

## 🎯 Business Objective
The analysis aims to help telecom businesses:
* Identify customers who are more likely to churn.
* Understand the major factors associated with customer churn.
* Develop targeted customer retention strategies.
* Reduce customer churn.
* Improve customer retention and customer lifetime value.
